        Leaning on the board

        How hard the board carves and trims for how far you lean. Centre is how much small movements count, max how quickly a bigger lean builds to a full turn: a low centre and a high max keep small wobbles gentle and still let you turn hard. The dashed line is the old straight response. You can change these in VR too, and see where your own lean sits on the graph.
